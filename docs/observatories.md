# Multi-Wavelength Observatories & Facility Registry

## 1. Overview

CELESTIAL catalogs premier ground-based and space-based astronomical observatories across optical, near-infrared, sub-millimeter, radio, X-ray, and gravitational wave regimes.

## 3. "What Can I Observe Tonight?" Engine

Each observatory profile calculates observable targets in real-time from its precise geographic coordinates, elevation, and local sidereal time:

- Computes culmination altitudes, transit timings, and airmass limits.
- Checks solar twilight and lunar separation.
- Ranks candidate targets according to observational quality heuristics.
